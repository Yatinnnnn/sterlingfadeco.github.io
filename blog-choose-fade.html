Choose a Fade That Fits Your Style

September 4, 2026 · 3 minute read

The height of a fade changes the balance and contrast of your haircut. Knowing the basic difference makes it easier to explain the finish you want.

Low fade

The transition sits closer to the ear and neckline, keeping more weight through the sides for a subtle, versatile finish.

Mid and high fades

A mid fade creates noticeable contrast without feeling extreme. A high fade removes more weight and creates a bolder, sharper silhouette.

Taper and burst fades

A taper focuses on the temples and neckline for a softer result. A burst fade curves around the ear and works well with styles that keep length toward the back.

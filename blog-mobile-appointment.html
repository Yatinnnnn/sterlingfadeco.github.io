Prepare for Your Appointment

September 4, 2026 · 3 minute read

A little preparation gives your barber the best environment for a detailed, efficient service—without turning your home into a barbershop.

Choose a practical space

Select a clean, well-lit area with room around the chair. Hard flooring is ideal when available, and a nearby electrical outlet is helpful for equipment.

Start with clean, dry hair

Unless your service instructions say otherwise, clean hair makes it easier to assess natural growth, texture, and shape.

Keep your reference ready

If you have a particular cut in mind, save one or two clear reference photos. Your barber can then adapt the style to your hair and features.

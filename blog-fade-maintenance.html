How Often Should You Refresh a Fade?

September 4, 2026 · 3 minute read

A fade looks sharpest when the transition remains clean around the sides, neckline, and edges. The ideal maintenance schedule depends on how close the fade begins and how quickly your hair grows.

Skin and high fades

Because these styles create stronger contrast, regrowth becomes noticeable sooner. Many clients prefer a refresh every two to three weeks.

Low, mid, and taper fades

These styles can grow out more softly. A three-to-four-week schedule often keeps the shape polished while allowing some flexibility.

Choose consistency over a strict rule

Your lifestyle, hair texture, and preferred level of sharpness matter more than a universal schedule. A recurring appointment or membership makes it easier to maintain a consistent look.
